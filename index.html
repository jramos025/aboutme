Hands-on owner who plans ahead

Hi, I'm Justin. I run IT for one of the largest REALTOR associations in the Bay Area, I genuinely enjoy it, and I'm good at making technology feel a lot less intimidating for the people who rely on it.

I run IT the way a lean organization needs it run: someone has to set the direction and also be able to fix the problem when it lands. For five years, that has been me at Bay East Association of REALTORS®.

My path started in operations at Alcatraz Island, as part of the team that ran the island and shaped its visitor experience, managing vendor contracts and leading workflow improvements. Moving into IT let me combine that business mindset with the systems work I enjoy most: connecting platforms, tightening security, and building tools people actually use. I am a fast learner and always enthusiastic about new tech.

Alongside my role at Bay East, I advise a medical group in the Philippines on technology and innovation, working directly with its board.

I explain technical decisions in plain language, document what I build, and train staff and real estate professionals so fewer questions need to escalate. I bring that same ownership to whatever the role needs, from hands-on support to systems and infrastructure to security and AI adoption.
